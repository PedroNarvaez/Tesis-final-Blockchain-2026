## 2026-07-20 - Accessible and Clickable Configuration Inputs and Checkboxes
**Learning:** In complex enterprise dashboards, sidebar controls and filters often suffer from poor accessibility (missing htmlFor/id associations) and tiny click targets for checkbox/toggle inputs. Associating labels properly and making entire row blocks clickable with subtle hover highlights greatly enhances interaction convenience, especially on smaller screens or for keyboard-only/screen-reader users.
**Action:** Always link form labels to their inputs using htmlFor and unique IDs. For checkboxes or toggles, wrap the descriptions in label tags and make the container interactive/clickable so that clicking any descriptive text toggles the target checkbox directly.

## 2026-07-21 - Keyboard Navigable Custom Selection Lists and Toast Feedback
**Learning:** Enterprise worklists often present rows that behave as selectable lists but are implemented as un-focusable `div`s. This excludes keyboard-only users. Converting them into semantic focusable elements (or standard buttons) with `role="option"` inside a `role="listbox"` container allows screen readers to announce selection states properly. Coupling this selection list with a reactive non-intrusive Toast notification (`role="alert"`) ensures immediate confirmation of asynchronous actions.
**Action:** Implement worklist selections using `button` or `a` tags, add standard `role="listbox"` and `role="option"`, style focused states with distinct `focus-visible:ring-2` outlines, and provide success/info Toast messages to confirm execution.
