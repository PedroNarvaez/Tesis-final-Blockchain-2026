## 2026-07-19 - [Accessible Form Controls and Click Targets in Reconciliation Sidebar]
**Learning:** In highly complex SaaS dashboards where users frequently toggle operating scenarios or fine-tune rulesets, standard checkboxes and small numerical inputs without proper accessibility markers can cause user frustration and poor keyboard/screen reader compatibility. Transforming standard divs around options to semantic interactive `<label>` components with focus-visible indicators significantly enlarges touch/mouse click hit targets, improves keyboard navigation visual feedback, and guarantees screen-reader compliance with minimal code impact.
**Action:** Always wrap or associate custom check/toggle panels in a styled `<label htmlFor="...">` and style them with hover, focus-within, and keyboard ring styles.
